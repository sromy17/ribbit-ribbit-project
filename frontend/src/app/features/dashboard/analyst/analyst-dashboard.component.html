Metric definitions

Funding measures transfers, not profit. Net deposits = deposits minus withdrawals. Deposit sizes describe individual transfers; customers are deduplicated across accounts. Weekly customer counts are not additive.

Top stocks rank by executed shares, then order count and ticker, excluding stocks without executions. Popularity uses that period's top ten; cells show orders / distinct clients across all statuses, grouped by order submission, not fill day.

Outcomes use current fills, with rejected/cancelled status taking precedence. Share-fill rate = executed / requested shares; values above 100% can indicate source-data inconsistencies. Execution participation is the percentage of orders with any executed shares, including partial fills.

Participation = accounts submitting orders / accounts created by period end. Weekly rates use each week's end. Accounts are not unique people or a retention measure. Active means at least one submitted order; available cash excludes holdings.

Weeks start Monday UTC; boundary weeks may be partial and inactive weeks are zero-filled. Overview, order-size and support totals are all-time and ignore the period filter. Order coverage describes event dates, not the ETL cutoff.

Modes include all tied repeated sizes; all-unique sizes have no repeated mode. Disputes count orders, not all historical claims: one dispute per order, preferring an open claim, otherwise the highest dispute ID.
